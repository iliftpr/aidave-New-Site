04 — The build

Six phases.
Four days. All live.

Gated, phase by phase — each shipped to the live store and verified with a real browser before the next began.

0
Phase 0 — Foundation

The engine under the hood

Built osc-concierge, one WordPress plugin that powers the whole funnel, and stood up an owned GA4 property (G-Y81434VVNT) with affiliate sub-ID attribution. Seeded a 66-product single source of truth, published the affiliate-disclosure and editorial-methodology pages, and rewrote llms.txt to point only at live URLs.

osc-concierge pluginGA4 dual-tag66-product SSOT/affiliate-disclosure//editorial-methodology/
1
Phase 1 — Flagship content cluster

Own the sensitive-skin conversation

A full topical cluster: a sensitive-skin pillar, 5 ingredient pages, 3 head-to-head comparisons (bakuchiol vs. retinol as the flagship), and an upgraded routine guide with a shoppable module. Every claim ran through an adversarial fact-check that caught fabricated citations and unsafe language a rules-gate can't. Then 32 existing posts were auto-cross-linked into the cluster.

1 pillar5 ingredient pages3 comparisonsAnswer-first + schema32 posts cross-linked
2
Phase 2 — The quiz

Capture intent + an email in one flow

A dependency-free 8-question skin quiz at /quiz/ with a fail-closed own-first recommendation engine (own heroes win by default; pregnancy & sensitivity filters never relax). Server-side Klaviyo capture, stateless shareable result links, and a full GA4 event chain. Verified live end-to-end across three personas — result email lands in ~30 seconds.

/quiz/Own-first engineKlaviyo captureGA4 eventsE2E verified
3
Phase 3 — Named routines

Turn the answer into a cart

A /routines/ hub and named regimens rendered from the same product map, plus a "shop the routine" one-click cart that adds only in-stock, own SKUs — guest-safe, rate-limited, and honest about what it skips. One data object drives the routine page, the quiz result, and the email alike.

/routines/Shop-the-routine cartIn-stock own SKUs onlyCart add proven live
4
Phase 4 — Free tools

Utility that earns the email

Two crawlable, claim-safe tools sourced straight from the ingredient pages: an Ingredient Conflict Checker (32 pairs) and a Pregnancy-Ingredient Checker (17 ingredients). The pregnancy tool is structurally claim-safe — it never renders the word "safe" as a positive claim, only flags what's commonly cautioned, and keeps an OB-GYN disclaimer on every result.

/tools/ingredient-conflict-checker//tools/pregnancy-ingredient-checker/Claim-safe by design
5
Phase 5 — AI-search optimization

Get cited by the answer engines

A machine-readable /recommendations.json feed, explicit AI-crawler rules, and three trust pages (Clelia's profile, sourcing & standards, /reviews/). Fixed the site's entity name, built an OpenAI merchant feed of 972 products, and measured a 5-engine AI-citation baseline — the real scoreboard for the AI era.

08 — Steal this playbook

Want this for your business?
Here's the exact recipe.

The whole build was four prompts and two tools. You don't need to code. Point Fable at Firecrawl, name the competitor whose funnel you admire, and go phase by phase. Copy these, swap the [brackets], and run them.

1
Firecrawl
Scrape the playbook
Use Firecrawl to scrape the entire marketing funnel of [COMPETITOR], the leader in my market. Map their acquisition machine end to end: the content and SEO pages that pull people in, any quiz or diagnostic, the product or offer pages that convert, every email and lead-capture point, and any free tools. Give me a structured teardown — the page types, the path from stranger to customer, and exactly where and how they capture intent.
2
Fable
Map it to your business
I run [MY BUSINESS]. I sell [PRODUCTS OR SERVICES] to [MY CUSTOMER]. Take that competitor teardown and adapt their funnel to my business — keep the architecture that makes it work, but fit my catalog, my margins, and my brand voice. Give me a phased build plan: foundation, a flagship content cluster, a quiz or diagnostic, offer pages, free tools, and AI-search optimization. Flag every decision that needs my approval before you build.
3
Fable
Build it live, phase by phase
Build the plan on my live site one phase at a time. After each phase, verify it works on the real site with a browser before moving on. Lead with my own products, use honest and claim-safe language, cite only real data, and never fabricate a review or a claim. Show me each phase before you continue to the next.
4
Fable + Firecrawl
Measure & compound
Set up an AI-search citation baseline for my new pages. Using Firecrawl, measure how ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google's AI Mode cite me across a fixed set of the questions my customers actually ask. Save it as my day-zero score and re-run it every month so I can watch it climb.
What you need
  • Fable — the AI that plans, writes, and builds it for you.
  • A Firecrawl key — the research engine that reads the competitor.
  • Your own website — where it all goes live. No code required.
The rules that keep it legit
  • Lead with your own products or offers — never cannibalize yourself.
  • Real data only — no fabricated reviews, ratings, or claims.
  • Claim-safe language — especially anything health, legal, or financial.
  • Verify every change live before you call it done.
