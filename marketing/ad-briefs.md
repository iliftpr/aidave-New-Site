# iLift Paid Ad Briefs — Local Long Island Launch

> Copy-paste-ready creative + targeting for the local-SMB paid push.
> Funnel: **ad → landing page / instant lead form → CRM instant SMS → free audit (cal.com/ilift/automation-audit)**.
> Budget: **$2,000–2,500/mo** = ~$1,000 Google Search + ~$1,000 Meta lead ads + ~$100–200 retargeting.
> Lead verticals: **(1) Home services / contractors, (2) Dental & medspa, (3) Restaurants & local retail.**
> Prereqs before spend: Meta Pixel + GA4/GTM live on ilift.com · CRM (LeadConnector/GHL) wired for instant SMS · landing pages deployed (`/ai-receptionist`, `/workshop`, `/pricing`).

---

## Global settings

- **Geo:** 15–20 mi radius around East Meadow, NY (Nassau + western Suffolk). Exclude NYC boroughs unless testing.
- **Hero offer:** "Never Miss a Call" AI receptionist (strongest, most universal pain). Secondary: reviews, get-found-on-Google, workshop.
- **Primary CTA destination:** Meta → instant lead form (converts ~12% better than landing for cold local traffic). Google → `/ai-receptionist` (high intent wants detail).
- **Qualifying question (all lead forms, keep to ONE):** "What's costing you the most right now?" → [Missed calls / No-shows / Not enough leads / Bad/few reviews].
- **Lead form fields:** Name, Phone, Business name. That's it (3 max).
- **Speed-to-lead:** auto-SMS within 60 seconds: *"Hey {first} — Dave from iLift. Got your note about {pain}. Want me to text you 2 quick ideas, or grab a free 15-min look? — Dave"*
- **UTM convention:** `?utm_source=meta|google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={vertical}&utm_content={ad_id}`.

## META (Facebook / Instagram) — Leads campaign

**Structure:** 1 campaign (objective: **Leads**, instant form) → 3 ad sets (one per vertical) → 3 ads each.
**Budget:** ~$1,000/mo, Advantage+ placements, start ~$15/day per ad set, let it learn 4–5 days before judging.
**Creative format per ad:** GPT Image 2 static + 1 short Kai/Seedance video per ad set. No Higgsfield text overlays (gibberish) — add text in Canva.

## Creative production checklist
- Static: GPT Image 2 (`gpt-image-2-text-to-image` via Kai, or Higgsfield GPT Image 2). Keep one style across a vertical's set.
- Video: Kai (kie.ai) or Seedance 2.0 — 6–10s, vertical 9:16 for Reels/Stories + 1:1 for feed.
- Dave's face: Higgsfield Soul Character (ID `8d85afeb-5283-4e0f-8940-7a2e05119476`), Tier 2 editorial.
- Add ALL text overlays in Canva (Higgsfield/Kai render gibberish text).
- Keep local copies — KIE deletes generated files in ~14 days.

## What to watch (first 30 days)
- Target: 20–30 leads/mo at **$60–100 CPL** by month 3 (expect higher week 1–2 while learning).
- Kill any ad set/keyword > 2× target CPL after 50+ clicks or 5–7 days.
- Double down on the winning vertical + channel once one hits 50 leads.
- The #1 waste to avoid: optimizing to clicks instead of the **Lead** conversion event.
